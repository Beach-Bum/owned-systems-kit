# Company Profile

## About

Northstar Studio is a service design consultancy based in Amsterdam. We help mid-sized companies improve their customer operations through process design, documentation, and training.

## Founded

2019

## Team size

12 people

## Services

- Process design and documentation
- Customer operations consulting
- Training and workshops
- Operational audits

## Clients

We work with mid-sized companies (50-500 employees) in the Netherlands and Germany, primarily in professional services, logistics, and healthcare administration.

## Key policies

- All client data stays within EU infrastructure.
- We do not share client information between engagements.
- AI tools may reference our service descriptions and public pricing. AI tools may not access client-specific data without project-level approval.

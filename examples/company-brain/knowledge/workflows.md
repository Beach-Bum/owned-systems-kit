# Workflows

## Customer inquiry triage

When a customer inquiry arrives, we classify it (new lead, existing client, support request, partnership), summarize the context, draft a reply, and route to the right person. AI assists with classification and drafting. A person reviews before sending.

See: workflows/customer-inquiry-triage for the full workflow definition.

## Proposal drafting

When a qualified lead is identified, we extract requirements from the inquiry, match to our service offers, identify gaps, and draft a proposal outline. AI assists with extraction and matching. A partner reviews before the proposal goes to the client.

See: agents/skills/proposal-drafting/SKILL.md for the agent skill.

## Monthly process review

Each month, we review active client processes for stale documentation, missed reviews, and open issues. This is a human-led process. AI may summarize findings but does not make recommendations without review.

## Onboarding new team members

When a new person joins, they receive access to the company brain, complete the process documentation training, and shadow an experienced colleague for one week. Documentation access is scoped: new hires cannot access client-specific data until their project assignment.
